The Story Behind The Thread

How Soch Vastr Began

Some brands begin with a business plan. Soch Vastr began with a feeling: the search for something truly different, thoughtful, and made to stay close to the heart.

One fine day, I found myself struggling to find clothing that felt distinct from the ordinary choices available everywhere. That everyday search turned into a simple but powerful question: Why not explore this space myself and give people the absolute best?

People often say that after 40, you stop chasing new ventures. I believe age is just a number, and it is never too late to begin something meaningful. Coming from a small hometown, I wanted to create a trusted local brand where people would not have to wait days or compromise on quality.

That single thought planted the seed for Soch Vastr: a curated world of premium ethnic wear, handcrafted details, skin-soothing fabrics, and festive pieces chosen with genuine care.
