The Story Behind The Thread

The Soul of Soch Vastr

Soch Vastr ki shuruaat kisi bade business plan se nahi, balki ek simple se khayal se hui thi—kuch aisa talashne ki jo market ki aam bheed se alag ho, jo dil ke kareeb ho aur jisme asli parampara ki khushboo ho.

Aksar log sochte hain ki ek umar ke baad naye sapne nahi dekhe jaate, lekin mera maanna hai ki junoon ki koi umar nahi hoti. Apne ghar-parivar ke beech rehkar, maine yeh chhota sa prayas shuru kiya taaki har mahila ko bina kisi lambe intezaar ke sabse behtareen aur shuddh ethnic wear mil sake.

Aaj Soch Vastr sirf kapde nahi, balki ek bharosa hai—skin-soothing fabrics, haath ki naazuk karigari aur har tyohar ko aur khaas banane ka ek chhota sa zariya.
